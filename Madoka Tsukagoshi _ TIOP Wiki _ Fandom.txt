Madoka Tsukagoshi (塚越 円 /Tsukagoshi Madoka, or 正鬼円 Masaki Madoka/)
is SHSL Kibougamine Fangirl at the rebuilt Kibougamine Academy. She is
best known for for her stalking ability, and constant involvement in
major events.

Madoka is a descendant of the Masaki family who are in pact with demonic
parasites. She is also the granddaughter of Sadaaki Masaki and is
referred to among certain circles as Masaki-Hime or Demon-Hime due to
the common misconception that she was chosen as Sadaaki's heir.

incorporates auctioned ex-clothing of previous Ultimate Students, all of

embroidered this herself as a reference to past SHSL Hope talents.

kept her usual pink-maroon colour. She is usually seen in business-
professional suits, namely black-and-white theme consisting of blazers

constructions she wears in her youth; showcasing the growth in her

    Personality[<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Madoka_Tsukagoshi?
    veaction=edit&section=2>]

Although Madoka can appear rash and vulgar at surface level, she can be
rather sarcastic and cynical, also proving herself to be great observer
of human behaviour. At her core, Madoka is a deeply lonely person who
wants people to like her. In fact, her constant brushes with rejection
have ultimately lead her to a very deep-seated fear of abandonment.
Madoka desires normal relationships, but due to her extreme personality,
has struggled in the past with maintaining friendships. She has a habit
of developing deep obsessions with fantastical things, as well as a
tendency to cross boundaries with her antics; a forced product of her
'Fangirl' persona, which does seem to fade considerably over time.

Madoka is shameless, often open to expressing her thoughts and feelings;
both the good and the bad. She has a blunt, crass way of speaking that
is often considered inappropriate or offensive. Her blatant nature and
openness can also be a 'breath of fresh air' to others, as she is not
afraid to express her thoughts. A lot of her interpersonal conflicts
stem from her not being aware of the weight of her own words and
actions, leaving others exasperated, exhausted or annoyed.

Although she presents herself as very careless and outgoing on a surface
level, Madoka can be very calculating, especially when it comes to
getting information or planning something for self-gain. She’s
incredibly street smart, creative and has out-of-the-box problem solving
skills, often through sudden epiphanies. Madoka may grow cold or hold
grudges toward people in cases where she has been physically hurt (as
she is often too weak to fight back) or has felt greatly offended.
Despite this, Madoka has trouble saying 'no' to doing favours or lending
her talents to people, even enemies, as she ultimately seems to crave
validation, and cares about being valued by others.

In regard to her talent, although Madoka may know excessive amounts of
information on an individual, when getting to know them on a deep level,
she will often not bring up what she knows unless she is specifically
prompted on it. Like this, she often lets others reveal what they want
to share with her at their own pace.


    History[<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Madoka_Tsukagoshi?
    veaction=edit&section=3>]

From childhood, Madoka had difficulties associating with her peers. She
was often distracted, living in fantasy, and tended to see the world
differently to the other children her age. Because of this, and the
association her peer's parents had with Madoka's mother, she found it
very difficult to make friends throughout her childhood, and often
'tried too hard' to make friends, pushing the other children away from
her with her enthusiasm.

Sometime prior to her early school life, Madoka met Katsuo Harada
<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuo_Harada> with her father, Ryuuji.
<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Ryuji_Masaki> The two immediately bonded
over their technological gifts, and seeing Madoka make a friend,
prompted Ryuuji to ask Katsuo to 'take care' of his daughter; a burden
which would see Katsuo through for many years.

When Madoka was seven, she invited 105 people from her school to her
birthday party, which nobody showed up to. Seeing her distraught, her
parents decided to celebrate her birthday with her. She was able to show
her family the toy robot she'd constructed on her own, and was very
proud of it. This would later become one of Madoka's most treasured
memories.

Soon after this, Madoka was in and out of therapy after putting glass
shards in her classmate's pencil case, after a joking suggestion from
her father, /Ryuji Tsukagoshi, /when asking him how to deal with
bullies. Madoka continued struggle with friendships, but quickly
developed a higher interest in technology, due to her growing
hyperfixation; a long-running spy manga she read as a child.

At the age of nine, Madoka was already communicating online with
strangers, and this lead to an incident where she was kidnapped by an
individual known as Hamadachi, who had groomed her through an online
game. Madoka was kept tied up in his barn after being brutally assaulted
by Hamadachi, left to die on the freezing cold floor, hearing nothing
but the sounds of the animals- this triggering in her an intense fear of
them. Her family was quick to initiate a search, and she was found, with
the perpetrator promptly arrested.

Following this incident, Madoka's younger brother, Yuutaro, became
extremely attached to her- refusing to sleep in his own room and
constantly wanting to be around for her. This was numbed down shortly
after their mother constructed a small laptop containing AI copies of
herself, Ryuuji and Madoka for him to talk to in his room.

During the Tragedy, Madoka became very sick. In an attempt to help
Madoka, her cousin, /Shuu Tsukagoshi/ found his mother's alcohol stash
and gave it to her under the belief that it was 'special medicine'.
Madoka, intoxicated and suffering from hallucinations, ended up shooting
her parents and younger brother, under the impression that they were
intruders. This left Madoka's aunt, /Ikue Tsukagoshi/ heartbroken, yet
unaware that it had actually been Madoka who had pulled the trigger on
their family.

During the Recovery Period, Madoka lived with both Ikue and Shuu,
although the family seemed to be broken. Madoka and Shuu from guilt, and
Ikue from losing her loved ones. Madoka seemed to change in personality,
often dry, sarcastic and bitter toward others in her life. This attitude
only lasted for a short period of time, and half way into middle school,
Madoka developed an obsession with Kibougamine Academy.

Prior to attending Kibougamine herself, Madoka attended a school for the
technologically gifted, alongside Shuu, where the students were trained
in information technology, as well as many other areas in technology.

Her attitude brightened up, although she was badly bullied by her peers
due to her obsessions. This had a negative effect on Madoka, as her
self-hatred only grew. Pushing past it, she started to lash back at the
bullies aggressively with revenge tactics. Disgusted with herself,
Madoka cut her hair and varied her attitude until she was completely
different.

She began acting out, doing crazy things for attention, even going so
far as to encourage sexual exploitation directed at herself. Not long
after jumping into these habits, Madoka met /Keiji Handsome <https://
tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Keiji_Handsome> /and became sexually involved with
him. This she gave up after becoming pregnant with his baby at just
fourteen, and having to abort the child.

Despite Madoka being crushed by this, she resolved not to tell Keiji
about this, but merely cut off their relationships. Keiji, triggered by
her sudden abandonment, used his talent to force her to sleep with him
in order to make her stay.

Madoka attended Kibougamine after being scouted for her intense
enthusiasm involving the school.

      Yuutaro Tsukagoshi[<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/
      Madoka_Tsukagoshi?veaction=edit&section=5>]

Yuutaro was Madoka's younger brother. Despite Madoka having difficulty
with her peers, she was able to establish a firm and caring relationship
him. Yuutaro seemed to care and admire her above anybody else in the
world. From infancy, he would cling to Madoka, often play with her and
even refuse to sleep anywhere else but in a room with her, which Madoka
and her mother responded to by making interactive AIs (similar to Alter-
Ego) of the both of them and his father, for him. Madoka cared deeply
for Yuutaro, despite once acting like he was an annoyance as her younger
sibling, and would often dedicate special time to entertaining him.

During the Tragedy, Madoka shot and killed Yuutaro while hallucinating.
She feels immense guilt over his death.

    Trivia[<https://tiop.fandom.com/wiki/Madoka_Tsukagoshi?
    veaction=edit&section=8>]

  * The name Madoka is written out as the kanji for 'circle' (円)
  * The surname Tsukagoshi is written with the kanji (塚) meaning 'small
    hill/mound' and (越) meaning 'to pass/cross over'.
  * The surname Masaki is written with the kanji for 正 (correct/true/
    original) and 鬼 (demon/ogre/evil spirit), an allusion to Madoka's
    heritage.
  * Madoka's blood type is AB+
  * As a child, Madoka had suspected ADHD <https://en.wikipedia.org/
    wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder> but was never
    medicated. In many of her adult verses, Madoka takes medication for
    her ADHD, and exhibits many cluster B personality traits, especially
    those associated with Borderline Personality Disorder <https://
    en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderline_personality_disorder> (BPD).
  * Madoka's hobby is /hacking into camera feed./
  * Her favourite food is /fried eggs /and/cookies /while her least
    favourite is/olives./
  * Madoka likes /Kibougamine Academy/ and /manga /and dislikes/animals./
  * Her most treasured item was her /younger brother./
